
# Peptide Advertising Creative Workbook

This repository contains practical notes and creative planning resources for businesses operating in the peptide industry.

Advertising creative plays an important role in how a business communicates its products and services. Clear messaging and thoughtful visual planning can help brands present information more effectively.

## What This Workbook Covers

- Advertising creative concepts
- Visual content planning
- Ad copy development
- Audience-focused messaging
- Campaign preparation
- Creative review guidelines

## Creative Planning Process

### 1. Understand the Business
Identify the business model, target audience, and main communication goals.

### 2. Plan the Message
Keep advertising messages clear, relevant, and easy to understand.

### 3. Develop Visual Concepts
Prepare visual ideas that match the brand identity and campaign purpose.

### 4. Review Advertising Content
Check claims, images, messaging, and platform requirements before publication.

# Advertising Creative Review Checklist

Before publishing an advertising creative, review the following:

- [ ] Is the main message clear?
- [ ] Does the design match the brand identity?
- [ ] Is the text easy to read?
- [ ] Are images relevant to the message?
- [ ] Have unsupported claims been removed?
- [ ] Have platform advertising rules been reviewed?
- [ ] Is the final creative suitable for the intended audience?

A proper review helps maintain consistent and responsible advertising communication.
